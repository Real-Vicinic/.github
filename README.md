# VICINIC

DevOps and IT solutions for education institutions and small businesses in South Africa.

VICINIC builds, automates and runs the systems that education institutions and small businesses depend on. We handle the infrastructure, deployment, automation and support behind them, so clients get tools that work without needing an in-house IT team. The setup fee pays for the solution; the monthly fee pays for keeping it running.

**Web:** [vicinic.co.za](https://vicinic.co.za) · **Contact:** miguel@vicinic.co.za

## The problem

Education institutions and small businesses in South Africa's underserved communities run on paper, personal phones and guesswork, because the technology that would fix it is built and priced for big, well-funded organisations.

## What we do

- **Cloud and hosting:** setup, deployment and management of client systems
- **Automation:** bulk SMS, WhatsApp, payments and admin workflows
- **CI/CD and infrastructure as code:** repeatable, documented deployments
- **Monitoring, backups and security:** uptime, recovery and access control
- **Email and domain setup:** DNS, DKIM and deliverability
- **Web performance:** website optimisation and maintenance where a client's system needs it
- **Support and training:** ongoing help so clients can rely on what we build

## Who it's for

- **Education institutions:** schools, colleges, training centres and similar, for communication with learners, parents and staff, plus admin tools
- **Small businesses:** customer communication, sales, payments and admin systems

## Principles

- Built for access
- Automate the work, keep the people
- Clients own their data
- Local first

## Data and privacy

Client data stays the client's. We process personal information as an operator under POPIA, and we do not send unsolicited SMS, WhatsApp messages or calls to anyone who hasn't opted in.
